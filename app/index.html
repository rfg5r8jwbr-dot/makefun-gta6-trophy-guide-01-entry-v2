    Do not miss this one

    Positive Mental Attitude (Bronze) is the only trophy reported as possibly missable. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia suggests meditating on the balcony at Jason’s house. Follow her and accept, or the moment may be gone for good.

    It is still unconfirmed whether that Chapter 1 meditation is actually required for the trophy. PowerPyx’s author did it and then earned the trophy, so doing it is the safe move. Keep a manual save from the start of each chapter.

      Online trophies

      There are none. All 55 trophies are offline single-player, so this section is empty on purpose: nothing here needs PlayStation Plus, a connection, or another player. The whole platinum is earnable alone, with the network off.
